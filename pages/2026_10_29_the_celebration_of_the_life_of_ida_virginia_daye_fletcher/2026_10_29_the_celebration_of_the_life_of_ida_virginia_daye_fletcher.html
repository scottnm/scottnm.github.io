Obituary

Ida Virginia Daye Fletcher was born in Baltimore, MD to the late John H. and Hester E. Jones on May 18, 1913 and departed this life on April 19, 1998 at the Heritage Hall Nursing Home, Leesburg, VA. She moved to Loudoun County at a young age with her family. Ida was educated in the local school system and became a member of Providence Baptist Church.

She married the late Harvey E. Daye, Sr. in 1932 and from this union were blessed with 17 children. Ida worked in the Leesburg laundry. She was also a domestic engineer for several local families in the Virginia and Washington, D.C. areas. She was employed at the Watergate in Washington, D.C. from which she retired and later became a childcare provider for many years.

She worked diligently in the Providence Baptist Church and was consecrated as a deaconess in March of 1981. She was the Chairperson of the Flower Club, President of the Missionary Club, active member of the Deaconess Board, member of the Hospitality Club, Pastors Aide Club and senior choir.

She married James J. Fletcher, Jr. in 1973 and was married until his death in 1986. Nine (9) children and her only brother, John H. Jones, Jr. predeceased her in death. She leaves to mourn her death, six daughters: E. Isabelle Wood, Shirley M. Fletcher, Martha E. Hall all of Leesburg, Edith J. Hughes of Chantilly, Va., Mary E. Allen of Silver Spring, Md., and Dorothy M. Daye of Center of Centreville, VA; two sons: James E. of Dumfries, VA and Charles S. of Leesburg, VA. Thirty-six grandchildren, forty-three great-grandchildren, twelve great-great grandchildren, two dedicated sons-in-law, and a host of other loving relatives and friends.

Ida always loved helping anyone she could and lived by her favorite spiritual: "If I could help somebody along the way, then my living shall not be in vain." "Mama", "Mama Ginny", "Granny", "Gran", "Miss Virginia", and "Nannie" as she was commonly known by many who shared her life will be greatly missed for a long time to come.

Visitation will be at the Loudoun Funeral Chapel 158 Catoctin Circle Leesburg, VA. on Wednesday, April 22, 1998 from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m. Services will be held at the First Mt. Olive Baptist Church on Thursday, April 23, 1998 at 11:00 a.m. Officiating will be the Rev. Herbert C. Love. Interment will be in the Mt. Zion Community Cemetery Leesburg, VA.
